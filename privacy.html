Backgammon Tournament

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Backgammon Tournament (“the app”) is published by Mercator Geosystems BV, the Netherlands (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the app handles, where it goes and what you can do about it.

In short

  • The app has no account of its own and never asks for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location.
  • The app contains no analytics and no advertising or tracking software from other companies. It does not track you across apps or websites.
  • Playing against the computer works without any personal information leaving your device.
  • Online play uses Apple’s Game Center and iCloud. For that, your Game Center player ID, your Game Center nickname and your rating are used, and some of it is stored on Apple’s servers.

Information that stays on your device

The app saves the following on your device only:

  • your settings (sound, board theme and similar choices) and the name you enter for games on this device;
  • your progress in Career mode and in the lessons;
  • your ladder rating, your recent match results and your win and loss counts;
  • private rooms you have played in: the five-letter room code and your friend’s Game Center nickname.

Deleting the app deletes this information.

Information used for online play

Online features are optional. They work only when you are signed in to Game Center on your device.

Game Center (Apple). The app receives your Game Center player ID and nickname from Apple. It uses them to:

  • find an opponent and connect the two of you for a match;
  • show your nickname and rating to your opponent, and theirs to you;
  • post your rating to the app’s Game Center leaderboard, where other players can see your nickname, rating and rank.

During an online match the app sends your opponent your nickname, your rating, the dice rolls and your moves. There is no chat, and no messages, voice or pictures are sent.

Ladder challenges (Apple iCloud). When you challenge another player, or answer a challenge, the app stores a record in its iCloud database. A record contains:

  • the Game Center player IDs and nicknames of both players;
  • both players’ ratings at that moment, and the rating points at stake;
  • the date and time;
  • the answer (accepted, declined or forfeited).

These records are stored on Apple’s servers so that the other player’s device can receive them. The database is shared by all players of the app: the app only ever looks up the records addressed to you, but the records are not encrypted for one recipient and should not be considered private. They are kept until we delete them. You can ask us to delete the records that concern you (see “Your choices and rights”).

Notifications. If you allow notifications, Apple delivers a notification to your device when someone challenges you or answers your challenge. The app does not send marketing notifications. You can switch notifications off in the Settings app at any time.

Advertising and purchases

The app shows no advertising and offers nothing for sale. It contains no advertising software from other companies and shares no information about you with advertisers.

If a later version adds advertising or a purchase, this policy will be updated before that version reaches you. Purchases would be handled by Apple’s App Store: Apple takes the payment, and the app only learns from Apple whether the purchase has been made.

Who receives your information

  • Apple, which operates Game Center, iCloud and the notification service. Apple handles this information under its own privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
  • Other players, who can see your Game Center nickname, rating and rank, and the challenges between you and them.

We do not sell your information and do not share it with anyone else.

Where your information is stored

Information used for online play is stored on Apple’s servers, which may be located outside your country, including outside the European Economic Area.

Why this information is used

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation, the information described above is used because it is necessary to provide the online features you choose to use (performance of a contract), and for our legitimate interest in running a fair rating ladder.

Your choices and rights

  • Play without sharing anything: stay signed out of Game Center, or simply use the modes against the computer.
  • Notifications: switch them on or off in the Settings app.
  • Your Game Center profile: change your nickname and who can see your profile in the Settings app under Game Center.
  • Access, correction and deletion: you can ask us what information about you is stored in the app’s iCloud database, and ask us to correct or delete it. Write to the address below and include your Game Center nickname so we can find your records.
  • Complaints: if you are in the European Economic Area you may also complain to your data protection authority. In the Netherlands that is the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

Children

The app is a board game suitable for all ages and is not aimed specifically at children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Game Center accounts are managed by Apple and are subject to Apple’s own age rules and parental controls.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date at the top.
