The app has its own Help screen: press the question mark on the main menu. It explains the rules of backgammon, the controls and every game mode, and it has lessons you can play through. The most common questions are answered below as well.

Playing

How do I move a checker?

Checkers that can move glow. Tap one and it moves by the left-hand die. Tap the dice before your first move to swap them. To choose the destination yourself, drag the checker; the points it may land on light up.

Why can’t I move the checker I want?

The usual reasons: you have a checker on the bar that must come in first; the move would leave your other die unplayable when both could be played; or only one die can be played and it must be the higher one. Tap the lightbulb to see a legal move.

How do I end my turn or take a move back?

Your turn does not end by itself: press ✓ when you are happy with it. Until then, tap the dice or the curved arrow to take your last move back. If you would rather have turns end by themselves, you can switch that on in Settings.

Where did my turn go?

If a roll has no legal move, your turn is skipped and the status line says so. If your roll had only one possible play, it was made for you.

Are the dice fair?

Yes. Every roll comes from the device’s random number generator, for you and the computer alike, at every level. The computer never knows a roll before it is thrown; stronger opponents simply choose better moves.

Can I play sideways, on an iPad or on a folding iPhone?

Yes. Hold the device either way; the board rearranges itself and the game carries on.

Online play

What do I need to play online?

An internet connection and a Game Center account that is signed in on your device (Settings app, Game Center). Career, Quick Match and the lessons work without either.

How do I play against a friend?

Open Play a Friend, create a private room and send your friend its five-letter code. They enter it under Join, and the match starts once you are both in the room. You do not have to be Game Center friends. Friend matches do not change your rating.

How does Play Online work?

Opening it starts looking for a random opponent at once; the cross stops the search. It is always a single game without the doubling cube. Each turn has a time limit, shown as a ring next to your name. If time runs out, a random legal move is played for you. Leaving an unfinished online match counts as a loss.

How is my rating worked out?

Your rating is the average of your last 20 match scores. A win scores your opponent’s rating plus 400; a loss scores it minus 400. Everyone starts at 1000. Each new result pushes your oldest one out of the average, so beating a stronger player counts for more than beating a weaker one.

How do challenges work?

On the Leaderboard you may challenge any player ranked above you whose rating is at most 200 points higher than yours. The other player can accept or decline. Everyone has 3 declines every 7 days, and a challenge left unanswered for 48 hours uses one. Once a challenge is accepted, both players press Play on the Leaderboard and the match starts as soon as both are there.

I do not get a notification when someone challenges me.

Check that notifications are allowed for Backgammon Tournament in the Settings app, and that you are signed in to both Game Center and iCloud on the device.

How do I change my name?

For games on your own device, change it in the app’s Settings. Online, other players see your Game Center nickname, which you change in the Settings app under Game Center.
